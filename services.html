WHAT WE OFFER

Our services

Everything we install is done to standard, the first time.

CCTV Installation

We provide and install CCTV camera systems for residential and commercial properties across Helderberg and Cape Town. Every installation is set up for remote viewing so you can monitor your property from your mobile device, wherever you are. We'll advise on camera placement to maximise coverage and eliminate blind spots.

Alarm Systems

Intrusion detection systems including door and window sensors, motion detectors, and alarm panels. We tailor each system to the layout and usage patterns of the property. An armed response company of your choice can be integrated into the system.

Electric Fencing

Perimeter electric fence installation and repairs for homes and commercial properties. We also handle fault-finding and maintenance on existing fences.

Access Control

Gate and garage door motor installation, intercom systems, keypad and card access control for residential gates and commercial entry points. We install and program the system and show you exactly how to manage users and access levels.

General Electrical

General electrical installation and fault-finding work for homes and businesses. Includes DB board upgrades, circuit additions, plug and light point installation, and geyser wiring. All electrical work is carried out by a qualified electrician.
