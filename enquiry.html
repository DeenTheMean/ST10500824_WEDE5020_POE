FAQ

Common questions

Do you provide a free quote?

Yes. All quotes are free and come with no obligation. Fill in the enquiry form or contact us directly and we'll assess your requirements before providing a detailed quote.

What areas do you service?

We cover the Helderberg area as well as Cape Town, Stellenbosch and the surrounding winelands.

How long does a typical installation take?

It depends on the scope of work. Small to medium jobs won't take longer than a day or two. We'll give you a time estimate when we quote.

Are you registered and qualified?

Yes. Vision Security Systems is run by a qualified technician and is registered with PSIRA (Private Security Industry Regulatory Authority).

Do you offer a warranty on installations?

Yes. If something isn't right with the installation, we'll come back and fix it. Specific warranty terms are confirmed in writing at the time of quoting.

Can you work on an existing system?

Of course. We can extend, repair or integrate existing CCTV, alarm and electric fence systems regardless of who originally installed them unless they are severely outdated or defective.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept EFT bank transfer. Payment terms are confirmed per job and detailed in the quote.
